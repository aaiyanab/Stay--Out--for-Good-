Your Gift Transforms
Lives & Communities.

Every contribution — big or small — plays a vital role in empowering returning citizens to rebuild with dignity, purpose, and lasting hope. Thank you for believing in second chances.

Your Impact

Every Dollar Funds Real Change.

Your support helps us provide crucial resources — from faith-based curriculum and mentorship to life skills training and community connection — for individuals re-entering society.

By donating to Stay Out for Good, you help offer essential services that empower individuals to rebuild with dignity and purpose, and strengthen the communities they return to.

Programs Supported

What Your Donation Funds.

📚

Curriculum & Materials

Funding the 16-week re-entry course guides, participant handouts, and life group curriculum.

🤝

Mentorship Programs

Connecting returning citizens with dedicated mentors, sponsors, and faith leaders.

💼

Life Skills Training

Supporting employment preparation, financial literacy, housing assistance, and community engagement.

⛪

Church Ministry Support

Equipping and training local churches to launch and sustain re-entry ministries.

🏛

Prison & Jail Outreach

Extending the ministry inside correctional facilities to prepare individuals before release.

🌐

Community Events

Organizing gatherings, graduations, and outreach events that celebrate transformation.
